Privacy Policy

Study Time Tracker
Last updated: November 2026

Summary: Study Time Tracker is designed with privacy in mind. We do not collect, track, store on remote servers, or share any of your personal data or study history.

1. Information We Collect

None. Study Time Tracker does not require any account creation or registration.

  • We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account credentials.
  • We do not track your location, IP address, or device identifiers.
  • We do not use advertising networks, analytical trackers, or third-party SDKs that monitor user behavior.

2. How Your Data is Stored

All data created inside the app—including:

  • Study timer logs and session durations
  • Subject names and study notes
  • Daily goals and streak records
  • Theme and UI preferences

is stored locally on your device using private device storage. This data never leaves your device and cannot be accessed by us or third parties.

3. Third-Party Services

The app interacts with Google Play Services strictly for the following standard Android system features:

  • In-App Updates: To notify you when a newer version of the app is available on the Google Play Store. This is handled directly by Google Play and is subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Data Deletion

Because all data is stored exclusively on your device, you have full control over it at all times. You can permanently delete all app data by:

  • Clearing the app's storage in your device's Settings > Apps > Study Time Tracker > Storage > Clear Storage, or
  • Uninstalling the app from your device.

5. Children's Privacy

Study Time Tracker does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

6. Changes to This Policy

If we ever update this Privacy Policy in future versions, the updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
